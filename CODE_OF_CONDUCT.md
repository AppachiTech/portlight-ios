# Code of Conduct

Portlight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and accept
constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project
and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and
publishing other people's private information are not acceptable, in the
repository or anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately
through the contact details on the [AppachiTech](https://github.com/AppachiTech)
GitHub organization profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow
this code, and may restrict or ban participants, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
